2026-11-04 (fork AlexandreKoessler/pypStag)
	version 0.7.1: bent-box ('spherical') geometry, module constant
	stagData.SPHERICAL_CONVENTION = 2:
	  - .theta (colatitude), .phi (longitude) and .r in float64, computed straight
	    from the box coordinates instead of arctan2 of the float32 cartesian
	    coordinates (~1e-7 rad of error before); aspect and rcmb taken at the
	    decimal value of the par file;
	  - .vtheta, .vphi, .vr are the components StagYY writes in the bent box (since
	    cdff427); the cartesian .vx, .vy, .vz are now computed with float64 trig.
	Code that needs these conventions checks SPHERICAL_CONVENTION.
